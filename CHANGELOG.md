## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-01

### Added
- Multi-chain intent engine (Base, Arc, Arbitrum, Stellar).
- ZK policy circuits.
- Intent Router V2.
- Launch Factory V2.
- Support for x402 micropayments.
- Stellar passkey accounts.
- SEP-38/24/45 payment center.
- Cross-chain CCTP workflows.
- Allora AI price predictions.
- Webacy security scanning.

### Security
- EIP-712 signatures.
- Codehash verification.
- Egress guards.
- Prompt secret filtering.
